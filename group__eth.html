Detailed Description

Address parsing, RLP/keccak encoding and the JSON-RPC client.

The pure, host-testable parsers (eth_addr.h, eth_rlp.h, eth_json.h) plus the Keccak-256 digest (keccak256.h) and the HTTPS JSON-RPC client (eth_rpc.h). The parsers carry libFuzzer harnesses under fuzz/.
